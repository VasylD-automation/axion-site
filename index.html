FAQ

The things people ask us most.

Do you film with a camera?

Mostly no. The core visuals are generated with AI. If you need real footage, we work with your materials or arrange a shoot separately.

Can I run these videos as ads?

Yes. We deliver files in formats for Meta, TikTok, YouTube and Google Ads. If a platform requires an AI-content label, we'll show you how to add it.

How long does a video take?

A single video takes 2–3 days from script approval. A series of 5 takes 7–10 days.

Who owns the video?

You do. After full payment you get the source files and can use them without restrictions.

Do you take small one-off jobs?

Rarely. We're set up for brand, campaign and agency work, where the creative decisions matter as much as the render. If you need a single quick clip, we're probably not the most sensible choice.
